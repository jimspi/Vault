# Smart Recommendations System

Vault now includes intelligent recommendations that learn from your content and patterns to suggest relevant tasks, reminders, and topics.

## Features

### 1. Task Recommendations

The AI analyzes your documents and insights to suggest actionable next steps:
- Follow-up actions based on document content
- Tasks implied by your uploads
- Next steps for ongoing projects
- Actions to resolve contradictions or gaps

**Example:**
> **SUGGESTION**: Based on your Q4 planning document and budget analysis, consider scheduling a team review meeting to align on resource allocation.

### 2. Proactive Reminders

AI identifies time-sensitive items and action items from your documents:
- Deadlines mentioned in documents
- Follow-up dates for meetings or reviews
- Recurring tasks or check-ins
- Important dates or milestones

**Example:**
> **REMINDER**: Your product launch document mentions a March 15th deadline for design finalization. Consider scheduling design reviews soon.

### 3. "You May Like This" Suggestions

Based on patterns in your uploads and insights, AI suggests related topics:
- Topics that connect to your interests
- Related documents you might want to find or create
- Areas for deeper exploration
- Cross-domain connections

**Example:**
> **SUGGESTION**: You may want to explore customer retention strategies - your recent uploads on user feedback and engagement metrics suggest this could be valuable.

### 4. Pattern-Based Intelligence

AI learns what matters to you over time:
- Recognizes recurring themes in your documents
- Identifies your focus areas and projects
- Suggests connections between disparate topics
- Helps you discover insights across your knowledge base

## How It Works

### Manual Generation

When you click "Generate AI Insights":
1. AI analyzes ALL your documents and manual insights
2. Prioritizes recent uploads but considers full history
3. Generates 2-3 insights including:
   - Patterns across your content
   - Actionable suggestions and tasks
   - Proactive reminders
   - Related topic recommendations

### Automatic Daily Generation (7pm MST)

Every evening, Vault automatically:
1. Reviews what you uploaded or noted that day
2. Generates daily summary insights
3. Provides recommendations based on the day's content
4. Suggests follow-ups and related areas to explore

## Insight Types

### Pattern
Recurring themes or connections across multiple documents.

**Example:**
> All three recent documents emphasize mobile-first design, suggesting this is a key strategic priority.

### Suggestion
Actionable recommendations and "you may like" suggestions.

**Examples:**
- Based on your research notes, consider creating a summary document
- You may want to explore accessibility standards given your focus on user experience
- Consider scheduling a follow-up meeting based on yesterday's discussion notes

### Reminder
Time-sensitive action items and deadlines.

**Examples:**
- Q2 planning deadline is next Friday according to your uploaded schedule
- Follow up with marketing team on campaign results (mentioned in meeting notes)
- Annual review process starts in 2 weeks based on your calendar document

### Trend
Changes or developments in your knowledge base over time.

**Example:**
> Your focus has shifted from technical implementation (earlier uploads) to user experience and design (recent uploads).

### Contradiction
Conflicting information that needs attention.

**Example:**
> Budget document shows $50K allocation while project plan estimates $75K. These figures need reconciliation.

## Best Practices

### For Better Recommendations

1. **Regular Uploads**: The more content you add, the better the recommendations
2. **Manual Insights**: Add your own observations - they help AI understand your thinking
3. **Consistent Topics**: Upload related documents to help AI identify your focus areas
4. **Action Items**: Include tasks and deadlines in documents for better reminders

### Using Recommendations Effectively

1. **Review Daily Insights**: Check your automatic daily insights each evening
2. **Act on Suggestions**: Use recommendation insights to guide your next steps
3. **Set Reminders**: Convert reminder insights into calendar items
4. **Explore Suggestions**: Follow "you may like" suggestions to expand your knowledge

### Example Workflow

**Morning:**
1. Upload meeting notes and documents
2. Add manual insights about key decisions

**Afternoon:**
3. Generate AI insights to see patterns and suggestions
4. Act on task recommendations

**Evening:**
5. Review automatic daily insights (generated at 7pm MST)
6. Plan next day based on suggestions and reminders

## Privacy & Control

- All recommendations are based solely on YOUR content
- No external data or user comparison
- Your data never leaves your workspace
- Recommendations only visible to you
